4. 🎯 What exactly does AI find?

We shouldn't allow AI to invent random opportunities.

We'll define a set of growth opportunity categories.

Opportunity 1 — Checkout Drop
High traffic
+
Low checkout completion

AI investigates why.

Opportunity 2 — High View, Low Purchase
Product views ↑
Cart additions ↓
Purchases ↓

Possible hypothesis:

Product is attracting interest but failing to convert.

Opportunity 3 — Cart Abandonment
Cart additions ↑
Purchases ↓

AI investigates:

customer segment
device
price
time
payment method
Opportunity 4 — Customer Retention
First purchase ↑
Repeat purchase ↓

AI identifies customer cohorts with low repeat conversion.

Opportunity 5 — Payment Conversion
Checkout initiated ↑
Successful payment ↓

AI investigates payment-method/device/time patterns.

5. 🧠 AI Agent's actual job

This is extremely important.

AI is not the analytics engine.

Analytics engine

Calculates facts:

Conversion rate = 3.1%
Cart abandonment = 67%
Mobile conversion = 2.2%
Desktop conversion = 4.8%
AI Agent

Uses those facts to reason:

"Mobile conversion is 54% lower than desktop. The difference is statistically meaningful across the observed period. The strongest opportunity appears to be mobile checkout optimization."

So:

Analytics = WHAT
AI Agent = WHY + WHAT NEXT

🔥 This separation makes our architecture much stronger.
